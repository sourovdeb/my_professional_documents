# CELTA Assessment Dispute & Forensic Audit

## Overview
Cambridge CELTA assessment controversy and Sourov Deb's forensic audit process - both a professional setback and psychological turning point in his ability to advocate for himself and document his reality.

## Background: The Course
- **Program:** Cambridge CELTA (Certificate in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages)
- **Institution:** The ELT Hub
- **Candidate Agreement:** Signed 28 October 2025
- **Motivation:** "his own idea to change his life" - voluntarily walked back into formal education

## The Criticism Incident
- **Event:** Live feedback session in front of peers and students
- **Feedback:** "vibrant and likeable but lacking educational value", "get off your high horse"
- **Sourov's Response:** Held back tears, cried afterward
- **Trauma Trigger:** "me in the centre and everybody pointing finger at me and laughing" - childhood nightmare made real

## Course Completion & Assessment

- **Outcome:** Fail
