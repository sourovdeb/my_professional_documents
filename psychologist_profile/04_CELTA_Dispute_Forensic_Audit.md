The CELTA course aims to:

3. Demonstrate the ability to apply acquired knowledge in real teaching contexts with adult learners.

The course consists of **five units**:
1. **Learners and Teachers, Learning and Teaching Contexts**
2. **English Language Analysis**
3. **Key Skills (Writing, Listening, Speaking, Reading)**
4. **Lesson Planning and Resources**
5. **Professional Development for Teachers**

## 2. Assessment and Evaluation

### 2.1 Assessment Components
- **6 hours of teaching practice**, assessed on **42 criteria** defined by Cambridge.
- **4 written assignments** covering different aspects of English teaching.
- **Final evaluation** conducted by a **Cambridge University jury**.

### 2.2 Sanction
- The CELTA certification is a **university certificate** issued by Cambridge University, UK.
- It is registered in the **Répertoire Spécifique** under Article L.6113-6 of the French Labor Code.

## 3. Financial and Legal Terms

### 3.1 Payment
- **Total Cost:** €1980 net
- **Payment Terms:** Full payment due upon receipt of the invoice from The ELT Hub.

### 3.2 Cancellation Policy
- **30% Penalty:** If the trainee cancels **within 30 days of the course start date (January 5, 2026)**, 30% of the course fee (€594) is forfeited as compensation.
- **Force Majeure:** If the trainee abandons the course due to **force majeure** (e.g., illness, family death, pandemic), payment is **pro-rated** based on the time attended.
  - **Examples of Force Majeure:**
    - Medical leave (justified by a doctor's certificate)
    - Death in the immediate family
    - Course interruption by prefectoral decision (e.g., pandemic)
- **Non-Force Majeure Abandonment:** If the trainee abandons the course **without force majeure**, they must pay the **full course fee** as compensation for the costs incurred by The ELT Hub.

### 3.3 Governing Law
- The agreement is governed by **French law** (The ELT Hub is a French SARL).
- Any disputes will be resolved under **French jurisdiction**.

## 5. Dispute Resolution

### 5.1 Potential Disputes
- **Non-Completion:** If the trainee fails to complete the course, The ELT Hub may claim the **full fee** unless the abandonment is due to **force majeure**.
- **Refunds:** Refunds are only applicable in cases of **force majeure** or if The ELT Hub fails to deliver the course as agreed.

### 5.2 Legal Recourse
- Disputes are subject to **French courts**.
- The ELT Hub reserves the right to pursue legal action for **non-payment** or **breach of contract**.

## 7. Key Takeaways for Psychologist
- **Financial Stress:** The **€1980 cost** and **30% cancellation penalty** (€594) may contribute to financial anxiety, especially if the trainee faces **uncertainty about completion**.
- **Time Commitment:** The **140-hour course + 80-hour self-study** requirement may conflict with other **personal or professional obligations** (e.g., workplace conflicts, family responsibilities).
- **Force Majeure Protections:** The trainee’s **mental and physical health** (e.g., depression, family emergencies) are **legally recognized** as valid reasons for pro-rated refunds.
- **Legal Pressure:** The **French jurisdiction** and **full payment obligation** in case of abandonment may add **psychological pressure** to complete the course despite challenges.
